# Brand rationale

The mark shows three instruction tracks entering one controlled decision point and leaving through a single directional bracket.

That geometry reflects Skill Conductor's job: take scattered workflow knowledge, route it through one explicit skill contract, and produce a checked output for a target host.

The light and dark logos share identical geometry. The small icon removes the center dot to stay legible at compact sizes.
